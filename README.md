# Turn On the Light

An interactive page: a dark room of ghosts, ghouls and whispered doubts, and a cracked, glowing light switch. Turn it on.

**Open it:** https://pdlmnk.github.io/turn-on-the-light/

- Move your mouse (or drag a finger on a phone) to shine the flashlight.
- Click the switch to turn on the light; click again to go back to the dark.
- Birdsong plays when the light is on (speaker button bottom-right to mute).

Everything is in one self-contained `index.html` (fonts embedded), so it also works offline: just download and open it.
